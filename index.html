About Vyaasa

One point of coordination for maritime trade.

Vyaasa Trade & Logistix provides end-to-end maritime and logistics services with a focus on efficiency, compliance and reliability.

As Shipping Agents and Custom House Agents, we handle vessel operations, cargo clearances and regulatory formalities with professionalism.

Our services also extend to ship chandling, crew management support, and integrated logistics assistance, along with stevedoring and broking, helping coordinate the moving parts of shipping and trade.

We work with ship owners, charterers, importers and exporters, tailoring our support to the needs of each operation.
